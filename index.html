Who I Am

My Story So Far

I'm an Informatics student specializing in Artificial Intelligence at Ciputra University, and somewhere along the way, data became more than just a subject. It became the lens through which I see everything. It started with building apps. I shipped projects, wrote APIs, and trained models. But the more I built, the more I realized what fascinated me most wasn't the interface. It was the intelligence underneath it.

That curiosity eventually led me to publish research on AI-based disease detection, and it hasn't slowed down since. Then I discovered the Indonesian stock market, and something clicked. The idea that data could read market behavior, detect patterns, and inform decisions felt like the exact problem I wanted to spend my career solving. Machine learning wasn't just academic anymore. It had stakes. That's the direction I'm moving toward. Data, AI, and the systems that turn raw information into real decisions.
